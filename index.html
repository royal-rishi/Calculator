A LITTLE ABOUT US

A little less noise.
A lot more clarity.

Forma is a quiet space for your everyday math. No distractions, no account, just you and the numbers.

A little more power. Scientific mode adds trigonometry, powers, logarithms, and more. Tap DEG to switch to radians.

Percent, made simple. The % key divides a value by 100. For example, 200 × 10% = 20.

Yours, privately. History and your preferred theme stay in this browser. Clear them any time. Numbers are displayed to 12 significant digits; calculations use full precision.
